ClickPlan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Summary

ClickPlan does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data collected

ClickPlan stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Pen configuration (name, dose strength, doses per pen, clicks per dose)
  • Injection history (date, click count, estimated mg, remaining pen capacity)
  • Audio calibration parameters (click peak level, noise baseline)
  • App settings (language preference, plan mode)

This data is stored in Android SharedPreferences and never leaves your device.

Microphone

ClickPlan uses the microphone exclusively to count injection pen clicks in real time. Audio is processed locally and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

No internet access

ClickPlan does not require or use internet access. No data is sent to any server. There are no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party services of any kind.

No account required

ClickPlan does not require registration or login. No email address or personal identity is collected.

Medical disclaimer

ClickPlan is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Click counts and mg estimates are approximations based on your pen configuration. Always follow your doctor's dosing instructions.
