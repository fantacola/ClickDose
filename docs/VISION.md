# ClickPlan — Product Vision

## Mission

ClickPlan reduces user decisions, not just doses.

It is not a click counter. It is not a dose tracker. It is a **personal pen assistant**.

The user should ideally open the app once per week for about 30 seconds.
The app should tell the user exactly one thing:

> **This week: 37 clicks.**

Everything else should happen in the background.

---

## The weekly ritual

The user opens ClickPlan immediately before their injection.

1. The app shows one dominant instruction: **This week: XX clicks**
2. The user taps Start and holds the pen close to the phone
3. The app counts clicks automatically via microphone
4. At the target, the phone vibrates
5. The user saves the dose — done

The app is then closed until next week.

No planning. No calculation. No decisions.

---

## What ClickPlan reduces

| Without ClickPlan | With ClickPlan |
|---|---|
| Count clicks manually | App counts automatically |
| Calculate remaining doses | App tracks remaining |
| Plan dose reductions | App suggests at new pen |
| Worry about pen end | App reconciles automatically |

ClickPlan is not about adding features. It is about removing friction.

---

## The pen as a unit

A pen is a psychological and physical unit.

- Dose changes happen **only when a new pen starts** — never mid-pen
- The physical pen endpoint is the truth — if the pen stops before the expected click count, the app adjusts silently
- The pen number is tracked so that historical records are linked to a specific pen

This creates predictability. The user always knows what to expect.

---

## Clicks as the source of truth

mg values displayed in the app are estimates — calculated from click counts, pen strength, and calibration data.

Clicks are always the authoritative value. The app never asks the user to manually enter mg values after setup.

---

## Design philosophy

**Premium. Calm. Minimal.**

One number. One button. One ring.

Inspired by the simplicity of Apple Health and the calm confidence of Oura.
No gamification. No badges. No streaks. No social features.

The segmented circular ring is the central visual identity — representing remaining pen capacity at a glance.

---

## What ClickPlan is not

- Not a medical device
- Not a calorie or weight tracker
- Not a food-noise or BMI tool
- Not a community platform
- Not a subscription (data stays on device)
- Not a cloud service
