# ClickPlan — Product Vision

## The one-sentence pitch

ClickPlan tells you exactly how many clicks to count this week, so you never have to think about it.

## The weekly ritual

The user opens the app once per week, immediately before their injection.

1. The app shows one dominant instruction: **"This week: 37 clicks"**
2. The user taps Start and holds their pen to the phone
3. The app counts clicks automatically via microphone
4. At the target the phone vibrates
5. The user saves the dose — done in under 30 seconds

The app is then closed until next week.

## What ClickPlan reduces

ClickPlan reduces decisions, not just doses.

- No manual click calculation
- No manual planning
- No dose changes mid-pen
- No unnecessary input screens

The pen endpoint is the truth. If the physical pen stops before the expected number of clicks, the app adjusts — calmly, without error messages.

## Design principles in one line

Premium, calm, minimal. One number. One button.

## What ClickPlan is not

- Not a medical device
- Not a calorie tracker
- Not a weight loss coach
- Not a community platform
- Not a subscription service (for now)
